    HONOURABLE MENTION · FIELD NOTE

    20 September 2026

    THE DAY WE FINALLY MET AGAIN

    Six years of waiting. One very good Sunday.

    After years of phone calls, messages, different cities and repeatedly saying that we should someday go cafe-hopping, we finally got a day where the plan actually happened.

    20 September 2026 was that day. We went out, wandered around, talked about approximately everything, took enough photographs, and finally got to do the very ordinary thing that had somehow become a very long-awaited thing: just spend a whole day together.

    The Chill Cat IceCreamDaily Doze Bake HouseCafe Periwinkle

    A small photographic record of the day

    There was, of course, the food. And because neither of us apparently possesses the ability to estimate our own appetite with any accuracy, we did what any sensible pair of cafe-hoppers would do.

    We over-ordered!

    At Daily Doze, the confidence was high. The menu looked manageable. Our stomachs, however, had other plans.

    The Spaghetti Aglio Olio Incident

    Somewhere along the way, funny how you decided that I apparently have a very small appetite when compared to you and your roommates (very funny). A statement that aged rather badly.

    Because then came the plate of Spaghetti.

    We tried. We genuinely tried till 1:30pm. We pushed the same plate to each other (eat your veggies!!!). And despite all our confidence, all our previous food-related optimism, and all the mocking directed at my tiny appetite...

    we could not finish the spaghetti :(

    There is, fortunately, photographic evidence of the unfinished dish having exactly 1234 spaghetti strings (which you definitely agree lol). And also the ice tea never came to our table (thank god ~_~). History will remember this moment accurately.

    FIELD REPORT: OUR EATING CAPACITY WAS SIGNIFICANTLY OVERSTATED.

    But honestly, that was the best part.

    Nothing about the day needed to be extraordinary. We just needed a Sunday, a few cafes, too much food, enough time to talk, and the chance to finally be in the same place after all those years.

    20 September 2026, definitely worth the wait.
